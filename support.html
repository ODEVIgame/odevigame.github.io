Frequently asked questions

Playing

How do I play?

Fill the grid so every row and column holds each number once. Odd and even neighbours form islands, and corner numbers give island sizes. Learn ODEVI in 3 minutes walks through a whole puzzle, and the app has the same step-by-step tutorial under How to play.

I'm stuck. What now?

Tap Hint. It explains the next logical step in words and can fill it in for you. Check shows whether anything you have entered is wrong. You can also switch on the assists under the board: Show mistakes as I play and Show odd/even counts per row and column.

Does every puzzle really have one solution?

Yes. Every puzzle has been checked to have exactly one solution, and you can always reach it by logic, without guessing. The harder levels sometimes ask you to test a "what if?" in your head.

What do the green and red corner numbers mean?

A corner number turns green when its island is closed off and has exactly the right size. It turns red when something about that island is wrong.

How are the stars awarded?

One star for solving the puzzle, one for solving it without hints, and one for beating the par time for that level.

How do I start a puzzle over?

Open the puzzle and tap Restart puzzle under the assists.

Progress and updates

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Solved puzzles, stars, times and a puzzle in progress are kept in the app. Progress does not sync between devices, and deleting the app deletes your progress.

The app says "Update ODEVI". Why?

A newer version of ODEVI is required. Tap the button to open the App Store or Google Play and update; your progress is kept. If you're offline, the app simply keeps working until it can check again.

Is ODEVI available on Android?

ODEVI comes to the App Store first, with Google Play to follow. Both versions have the same puzzles and features.

Ads and privacy

Is ODEVI free?

Yes, ODEVI is free to download and play. From version 1.1 the app is funded by ads so that it can stay free.

When are ads shown?

Only between puzzles, never while you are solving one. Your first puzzles are ad-free, and only ads suitable for a general audience are shown. If you want extra hints for a puzzle, you can choose to watch a short video.

How do I change my ad and privacy choices?

Open Settings › Privacy choices in the app to change your consent at any time. On iPhone you can also control tracking under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you say no to personalised ads, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.

Does ODEVI collect my data?

Version 1.0 collects no data at all. From version 1.1, Google AdMob processes some data to show ads, and basic diagnostics help keep the app stable. There is still no account or sign-in. The privacy policy explains exactly what is involved.
