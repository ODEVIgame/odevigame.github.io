How it works

Three rules. One solution.

One clear rule set, exactly one solution per puzzle, from a two-minute warm-up to an hour-long expedition.

Every number once

Each row and each column holds every number exactly once, just like sudoku.

Odd and even make islands

Odd numbers are sand, even numbers are sea. Touching cells of the same kind (up, down, left or right) form one island.

Corner numbers are sizes

A small corner number tells you how many cells the island containing that cell has. Here every cell shows its size.

New to it? Learn ODEVI in 3 minutes walks you through a whole puzzle.

Made for thinking

Built to teach the logic, not just the rules.

Step-by-step tutorial

A guided 5×5 puzzle shows you how to reason your way to the answer.

Hints that explain

Stuck? A hint explains the next logical step, and can fill it in for you.

Assists, your way

Switch on mistake highlighting and odd/even counts per row and column, or play without.

Three stars to earn

One for solving, one for using no hints, and one for beating the par time.

Light and dark mode

Calm colours by day, deep sea by night. Easy on the eyes either way.

Plays offline

No sign-in and no connection needed. Your progress stays on your device.

Your privacy
