Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026 · Applies to the ODEVI app for iPhone and Android and to this website

In short

ODEVI has no account and no sign-in, and your progress stays on your device. ODEVI is free to play and is planned to be funded by ads. What data is involved depends on the version you have installed.

Version 1.0

No data collected

No ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Version 1.1 and later

Ad-supported, your choice

Ads are shown by Google AdMob. You decide whether ads may be personalised, and basic diagnostics help us keep the app stable.

You can see which version you have on the app's page in the App Store or Google Play.

1Who is responsible

ODEVI is developed and published by Jan Fredrik Nicolaisen, a private individual in Norway, who is the data controller for the processing described here. Contact: [support email].

2Data on your device All versions

So that you can carry on where you left off, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • which puzzles you have solved, your stars and your times
  • any puzzle you are in the middle of
  • your settings, such as the assists you have switched on, and your privacy choices

None of this is sent to us. It is deleted when you delete the app. Your device's own backups (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include it; those backups are handled by Apple or Google under your account with them.

3The update check All versions

When you open the app, and when you return to it, ODEVI downloads a small file from this website to see whether a newer version is required. The request contains no personal information and no identifier for you or your device, and the app keeps working if the file can't be reached.

Like any request to a website, the server that hosts this site may record technical details such as your IP address in standard server logs. We do not use these logs to identify or follow anyone, and we do not combine them with other data.

4Advertising Version 1.1 and later

Ads are what keep ODEVI free. From version 1.1, the app shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google. Ads appear only between puzzles, never while you are solving one. You can also choose to watch a short video ad to unlock extra hints. The app shows only ads that are suitable for a general audience.

To show ads, Google's software in the app processes:

  • technical data: IP address, device model, operating system and version, language, and app version
  • an approximate location derived from the IP address (country or region, never your precise location)
  • identifiers for the app installation, and the device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android), but only where you allow it (see your privacy choices)
  • information about the ads themselves: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped

Google uses this data to deliver the ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and, only if you agree, choose ads based on your interests and track ad activity across apps and websites from other companies.

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

5Your privacy choices Version 1.1 and later

  • Consent form. In Norway, the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent the first time it opens, using Google's consent tool. You can accept, decline or choose per purpose. Declining does not limit the game in any way.
  • Apple's tracking prompt (iPhone). The app asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not based on tracking.
  • Advertising ID (Android). You can reset or delete it under Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Change your mind at any time under Settings › Privacy choices in the app, or in your device's settings.

If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. Google then processes only the data needed to deliver ads, count them and prevent fraud.

6Diagnostics and usage statistics Version 1.1 and later

To find bugs and make the puzzles better, the app may send:

  • crash reports: what went wrong, device model, operating system and app version
  • usage statistics: for example which levels are played, how long puzzles take and whether hints are used

This data contains no name, email address or advertising identifier, is not used for advertising and is not sold. It is linked only to a random identifier for the app installation that is created inside the app and reset if you reinstall. [Name the service used, for example Firebase Crashlytics].

Where the law requires your consent for usage statistics, we ask for it in the consent form, and you can switch it off under Settings › Privacy choices.

8Who receives data

  • Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC), for ads and, if used, for diagnostics
  • Apple and Google as app stores, which give developers aggregated statistics on downloads and crashes under their own privacy policies
  • The company that hosts this website (see this website)

We never sell personal data. Some of these providers process data outside the EEA, for example in the USA. Such transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the EU's standard contractual clauses.

9How long data is kept

  • Data on your device: until you delete the app.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics: no longer than 14 months, and then deleted or kept only in anonymous, aggregated form.
  • Ad data processed by Google: according to Google's retention policy.
  • Emails: until your question has been handled and it is no longer needed, at most 12 months.

10When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you.

11Children

The puzzles in ODEVI suit all ages, but the ad-supported versions are not directed at children. The app shows only ads suitable for a general audience. Users below the age at which they can give consent themselves (13 in Norway) should have a parent or guardian make the privacy choices in section 5. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

12Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the app does not link data to your name, we usually cannot identify you in data held by Google. Use the privacy choices in the app, and for Google's data see My Ad Center. For anything else, email us.

You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

13This website

This website is hosted by [website host], which may process basic technical data, such as IP addresses, to deliver the pages. See the host's own privacy policy for details.

The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when you visit. The website itself uses no cookies, analytics or ads.

14Changes to this policy

If a new version of ODEVI changes what data is handled, we update this page before that version is released, and update the privacy details in the App Store and Google Play to match. The effective date at the top shows when the policy last changed.
